One feather.

Comb it sideways.

A feather drawn in fine barbs. It begins ruffled and brown. Combing it sideways brings its barbs into line.

Ruffled.

A finger or a pointer moved sideways is a comb; up and down only travels. With a keyboard, focus the feather, move along it with ↑ and ↓, and comb with ← or →. Left alone, it ruffles.
