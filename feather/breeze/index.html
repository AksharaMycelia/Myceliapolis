A feather drawn in fine barbs. It begins ruffled and brown. Combing it sideways brings its barbs into line.

Nothing to finish.

A finger or a pointer moved sideways is a comb; up and down only travels. With a keyboard, focus the feather, move along it with ↑ and ↓, and comb with ← or →. The breeze and the dog sometimes interrupt. Rest pauses the room; brushing still works. Reduced motion keeps it still, with the same buttons to change it.
