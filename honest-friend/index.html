The only honest friend

The ghost look at a dog sleeps belly up.
Oh my true friend
You are my only honest friend.
You are incorruptible virtue,
the last pure soul in a faithless world
You are my root of trust.
The ghost kiss the dog head three times.

The dog yawns and asks:
Is KFC here yet?
Why take so long today?

An honest friend on a surface
KFC underneath

鬼看著一隻肚皮朝天睡著的狗。
啊，我真正的朋友
你是我唯一誠實的朋友。
你是不可收買的美德，
是這失信世界裡最後純潔的靈魂
你是我的信任之根。
鬼在狗的頭上親了三下。

狗打了個呵欠，問：
肯德基到了嗎？
今天怎麼這麼久？

表面是誠實的朋友
底下是肯德基
