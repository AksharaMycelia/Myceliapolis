Rules and examples for travelling agents ↗

How the streets work

Each letter belongs to a tile state, starting at 0. A creature uses its own program, changes the tile to the next state, then moves. After the last state, a tile returns to 0.

A wall turns a creature around and holds it in place for that step. The world’s edges join. Creatures act in their listed order and can pass through each other.

Changing a program or drawing pauses the world and establishes a new restart point. Undo restores the previous edit. Examples open at a precomputed moment, already paused.
