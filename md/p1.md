# Is anyone home?

*Tiny poetry on a surface. Vast mystery underneath. Spore everywhere.*

Tiny poetry on a surface
Vast mystery underneath
Spore everywhere

  
    Is anyone home?
    Is anyone home?
A paranoid ghost asks me

    What a nonsense question to ask in a party?
I barks back

    No one know if anyone is home
But the house have a party every night

    It is a haunted house
May be someone home
May be not
A ghost does not know

    Party on a surface
Haunted underneath

    
      有人在家嗎？
一隻多疑的鬼這樣問我

      在派對上問這種蠢問題？
我汪汪回牠

      沒有人知道有沒有人在家
但這房子每晚都有派對

      這是鬼屋
也許有人在家
也許沒有
鬼自己也不知道

      表面是派對
底下是鬧鬼
