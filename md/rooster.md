# சேவல்

A column of yards around the earth, east at the top. Each shows its own hour, and what its rooster says.

    コケコッコー
    喔喔喔
    เอ้กอีเอ้กเอ้ก
    கொக்கரக்கோ
    قوقولی قوقو
    кукареку
    κικιρίκου
    kikeriki
    gaggalagó
    cocoricó
    quiquiriquí
    cock-a-doodle-doo
    〜

Where it is dawn, one crows without being asked. The yard to the west hears it. Before its own dawn it always answers; by day sometimes; at night seldom. So the cry runs west ahead of the sun, into the dark, and stops.

Press any yard and it crows, at whatever hour. That cry is passed along the same way.

At the bottom and the top is open water. No cry has been seen to cross it. Dawn does, every day.

The rule is in [/rooster/ring.mjs](/rooster/ring.mjs).
