# Cathedralis sine nomine

Room III, under the floor: https://myceliapolis.com/fungi/iii/

Ten threads hang in the dark, each a chain of small lights between two feet.
Their shapes come from weight alone. Turn the room over ("inverte") and the
same shapes stand as arches. One thread is strung nearly tight and stays low.

Touch a light and it lets go for a few seconds: its part of the rib goes slack
and the lights either side pull shorter to carry it. It also says something.
Most deny:

- Nullus chorus est.
- I don’t know the one beside me.
- เสียงหึ่งนั่นคือสายไฟ
- இது கோயில் அல்ல.
- 像教堂？那是你看出來的。
- न कश्चित् सङ्घः।
- È solo l’impianto elettrico.
- べつに、歌ってなんかいない。
- Nihil aedificamus.
- Any resemblance is yours.

Three do not. One says the acoustics are terrible. The low thread says it is a
kitchen. One, at the top of the tallest arch, says "It is a choir," and does
not let go.

The physics is in `/fungi/vault.mjs` (`raise`, `settle`, `letGo`, `nearest`,
`sayingOf`) and runs anywhere. The feet, lengths and sayings are authored; the
curves are not. Nothing is stored or sent.
