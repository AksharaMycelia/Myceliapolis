How the ground works

Both worlds use the Small Machines rules. Each creature reads its tile, turns according to its program, changes the tile, and moves one square. A wall turns it around and holds it for that step. The edges join. Creatures act in their listed order.

The left world continues without your edit. The right world begins with one changed rule or tile. They advance by the same number of steps. Rewind keeps your edit; Remove my change restores the arrival. The orange marks show different tile states, not a score.

Motion starts only when asked and pauses when this tab is hidden. With reduced motion, 64 steps are calculated at once. A malformed link is never silently replaced by a seed.
